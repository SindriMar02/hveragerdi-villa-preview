Hveragerði · South Iceland

Hveragerði

Two black timber houses on the river, half an hour from Reykjavík. Room for the whole family, a hot tub over the water, and trails from the door.

Both houses are a few minutes' walk from the town's park, with swimming pools five minutes away and the Golden Circle and the South Coast a short drive from the door.

The Deck Villa

A glass gable over the river, and a deck built for long evenings.

  • 4 bedrooms · up to 10 guests
  • Hot tub on the deck
  • Fire pit by the river
  • Granite island kitchen

Large windows look out on the river and the mountain. Trails start outside the door, the town's parks are about a five-minute walk, and there is plenty of private parking in front of the house.

4.99 from 193 guest reviews on Airbnb, read 19 November 2026.

The Riverbank Villa

A main house and a guest house, with a hot tub right on the riverbank.

  • 6 bedrooms · up to 10 guests
  • Main house + guest house
  • Year-round hot tub on the bank
  • Sonos · Apple TV

About a five-minute walk to the town's public park, with shops and restaurants within reach on foot. A fully equipped kitchen, a working corner and fast Wi-Fi.

4.95 from 78 guest reviews on Airbnb, read 19 November 2026.
